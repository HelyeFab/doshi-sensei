# 90-Day SEO Growth Plan – doshisensei.com
_Date: Aug 13, 2025_

---

## Phase 1 – Foundation & Keyword Strategy (Weeks 1–4)
Goal: Define search targets, set up content framework, optimize existing assets.

**Week 1 – Keyword & Competitor Research**
- Identify primary keyword clusters:
  - JLPT levels (e.g., “JLPT N5 verbs list”)
  - Grammar points (e.g., “～そうです grammar”)
  - Kana & kanji learning topics
  - Japanese learning tools (e.g., “Japanese verb conjugation tool”)
- Use: Google Keyword Planner, Ubersuggest, Ahrefs free, “People Also Ask”.
- Competitor scan: Tae Kim, Tofugu, JapanesePod101, LingQ — find content gaps.

**Week 2 – Site Structure Planning**
- Group keywords into topic clusters.
- Map out pillar pages and supporting content.
- Example:
  - Pillar: `/japanese-verb-conjugation-guide/`
  - Supporting: `/godan-verbs/`, `/ichidan-verbs/`, `/te-form-guide/`

**Week 3 – On-Page SEO Audit**
- Add missing meta titles/descriptions.
- Add alt text to images.
- Add internal links between related content.
- Set canonical tags where needed (`/favourites/`).
- Remove/redirect 404s (`/katakana`, `/kana`, `/reading-routes`).

**Week 4 – Technical Enhancements**
- Ensure sitemap lists only public pages.
- Add sitemap link to `robots.txt`.
- Test key pages in PageSpeed Insights & Lighthouse — fix image compression, preload fonts.

---

## Phase 2 – Content Creation & Authority Building (Weeks 5–8)
Goal: Publish high-quality, keyword-optimized content, start earning links.

**Week 5 – First Pillar Page**
- Write “Complete Guide to Japanese Verb Conjugation” (2,000+ words).
- Add diagrams, examples, internal links.
- Include FAQ schema.

**Week 6 – Quick-Win Content**
- Publish 3–4 low-competition posts (500–800 words).
- Examples: “Easiest way to learn katakana”, “Difference between は and が”.
- Share in Reddit LearnJapanese, Facebook language groups.

**Week 7 – Backlink Outreach – Round 1**
- Contact 5–10 Japanese learning bloggers/YouTubers.
- Offer free DoshiSensei access for review.
- Pitch guest posts.

**Week 8 – Internal Linking Boost**
- Use Screaming Frog/Sitebulb to find orphan pages.
- Add contextual links from high-traffic to low-traffic pages.

---

## Phase 3 – Scaling & Link Building (Weeks 9–12)
Goal: Increase topical authority and backlink profile.

**Week 9 – Second Pillar Page**
- Topic: “Learn Katakana – Complete Guide”.
- Include downloadable PDF cheat sheet.

**Week 10 – Backlink Outreach – Round 2**
- Guest posts on education & Japan travel blogs.
- Submit to educational resource directories.
- Ask teachers to list on school resource pages.

**Week 11 – Content Refresh**
- Update Week 5 content with new examples, change year in title.
- Add video or audio for better engagement.

**Week 12 – Data Review**
- In GSC: Improve low-CTR high-impression pages.
- In GSC: Check newly indexed pages.
- In GA: Identify top referrers, double down on what works.

---

## Ongoing Habits After 90 Days
- Publish 1 high-value article every 2–3 weeks.
- Quarterly backlink outreach.
- Monthly GSC performance review.
- Keep social media active.

---

💡 Special Tip for DoshiSensei:
Since your app is unique, create interactive content Google can index — e.g., “Interactive Verb Drill” with an accompanying SEO-friendly article.
